AI Use &
Tool Disclaimer

Oney & Co tools are designed to help Australians understand lending readiness, pressure points, and questions worth asking before they apply. Some tools may use rule-based logic, calculations, structured prompts, or AI-assisted wording. None of them approve credit or replace a licensed assessment.

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Short version

Our tools provide general information, indicative calculations, and lender-readiness guidance. They do not give personal financial advice, credit advice, legal advice, tax advice, or a lender decision. A lender, broker, adviser, accountant, solicitor, or other qualified professional must review your actual documents before any real decision is made.

How AI may be used

  • Summarising user inputs into clearer plain-English explanations.
  • Generating checklists, follow-up questions, or educational wording.
  • Classifying readiness signals such as Green / Amber / Red.
  • Helping compare policy pathways at a high level.

What AI does not do

  • It does not approve, decline, or recommend a specific loan.
  • It does not verify your documents unless explicitly stated.
  • It does not know every lender's current appetite or unpublished exceptions.
  • It does not replace responsible lending checks or broker compliance obligations.

Data and privacy

Only enter information you are comfortable sharing. Avoid uploading or entering unnecessary sensitive information unless a tool specifically asks for it. Where a tool includes lead capture or email follow-up, the page should explain what is collected and why.

Source freshness

Lender policies, interest rates, government rules, tax settings, and regulator expectations can change quickly. We aim to keep tools current, but outputs should be treated as a starting point and checked against current policy before relying on them.

For brokers and professional users

Oney & Co tools are workflow aids. They do not replace NCCP obligations, responsible lending inquiries, best-interests duty where applicable, credit-guide disclosures, privacy obligations, file notes, document verification, or lender-specific submission requirements. If a tool helps draft wording, review and edit it before using it with a client or lender.

Questions

If something looks wrong, stale, or too confident, do not rely on it silently. Contact hello@oneyco.com.au or speak with a qualified broker/adviser before acting.
