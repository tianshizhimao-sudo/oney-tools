Non-bank pathway view

Major-bank rejection does not always mean the deal is dead. It usually means the file needs a different evidence standard, pricing expectation, LVR limit, or exit story. Pepper is shown first because it is often the practical near-prime / specialist benchmark before testing other non-bank options.

Pepper MoneyFirst specialist benchmark for near-prime, alt-doc, credit impairment, complex income and commercial scenarios. Still evidence-led; not an approval shortcut.
BluestoneUseful where bank policy is tight on company/trust, genuine savings, self-employed or near-prime residential files.
La Trobe FinancialSpecialist pathway for SMSF, commercial, construction/private-style complexity and flexible documentation cases.
ThinktankStrong commercial/SMSF/property specialist; useful when security and DSCR story matter more than vanilla bank fit.
Liberty FinancialBroad non-bank fallback for complex borrower profiles, alt-doc and non-standard credit stories.
Boundary: this view is a policy navigation aid only. It does not recommend a product, promise lender appetite, or replace a broker/lender assessment.
