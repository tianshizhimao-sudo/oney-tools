About Oney Tools

Free, practical financial tools built for Australian borrowers and mortgage professionals.

What Are These Tools?

Oney Tools is a collection of calculators and assessment tools designed to help you understand borrowing capacity, evaluate deals, and navigate the lending landscape — without the jargon.

Who Are These Tools For?

Borrowers

First home buyers, investors, and property owners who want to understand their financial position before speaking with a broker.

Mortgage Professionals

Brokers and bankers who need quick, reliable calculators for client conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. All tools on this site are completely free to use. No sign-up required.

Is my data private?

All calculations happen locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, and we do not collect or store any information you enter.

How accurate are the calculations?

Our tools use standard lending formulas and current market assumptions. Results are indicative and should be used as a starting point, not a definitive assessment. For complex situations, always verify with a licensed professional.

Who built these tools?

Oney Tools is built and maintained by Oney & Co, an Australian financial services company. Our team has 8+ years of experience in business and mortgage banking.

Can I use these tools for client work?

Yes. These tools are designed to be practical for professional use. We ask that you verify outputs independently before presenting to clients.

How often are the tools updated?

We update our tools regularly to reflect changes in market rates, APRA guidelines, and lending policies. Check back for the latest versions.

Do you offer an API or embeddable version?

Not at this stage. All tools are available for free use via this website.

I found an error. How do I report it?

Please contact us at hello@oneyco.com.au with details of the issue.
