      ⚠️ SMSF Lending Notice

      Important: SMSF (Self-Managed Superannuation Fund) lending is subject to strict regulations under the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993.

      Key considerations:

      • Must be a "limited recourse borrowing arrangement" (LRBA)
      • Property must be held in a bare trust
      • Cannot be lived in by members or related parties
      • Limited lender options available
      • Higher rates and stricter LVR requirements
      • Single acquirable asset rule applies
      Recommendation: Please consult with your accountant and confirm lender appetite before proceeding.
