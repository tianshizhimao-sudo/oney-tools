Is your pre-approval still worth relying on?

A quick, general-information recheck signal for buyers relying on borrowing numbers that may be out of date.

Your cash rate didn’t change. Your approval might have.

A pre-approval is not a permanent borrowing limit. Even when the cash rate is unchanged, lenders can still reassess your income, debts, expenses, deposit, DTI position and policy fit.

This is not a borrowing calculator and it is not credit advice. It simply helps flag whether your numbers may need a fresh broker-style recheck before you make an offer. You can use the tool without submitting contact details.
