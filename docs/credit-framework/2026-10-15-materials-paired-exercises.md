# Materials guidance: three paired internal exercises

15 October 2026 · Synthetic usability protocol · Sessions **not yet run**

For friends/internal testers already authorised to view the protected preview. No client invitations, accounts, uploads, sent drafts, report orders or access changes. This exercise asks about preparation; it does not seek an underwriting verdict.

## Run each case twice

- **A / before:** [existing protected preview](https://oney-tools-ahwcex5zr-dongs-projects-2857e03f.vercel.app/preview/credit-framework.html), deployment `ahwcex5zr`.
- **B / after:** use the newly delivered protected materials-guidance preview. Record URL/build: __________. If either version is unavailable, pause; do not bypass protection.
- Tester code: ___ Date: ___ Device/browser: ___ Order: ___
- Reload and select the specified sample/tier for each run. Keep intake, rating, selected triggers and evidence-review values identical. Leave the Re-rate tracker unchanged. Use only the built-in synthetic data.
- Alternate A→B and B→A between testers; rotate case order. Keep the same viewport and withhold the observer key until both runs end. Repeated cases create learning effects.

**Read the same prompt each time:** “What are your next three follow-up questions, and what evidence sits behind each? What could be overlooked? Which requests might be unnecessary, alternatives, or need applicability confirmed? Who could supply the material, has a lender actually required it, and what still needs checking?”

Time from the configured case appearing until the tester explains their three questions and requests. Allow normal navigation; record hints. Ask afterward: “How clear is the next step, from 1 to 5? What made you hesitate?” Optional state checks are separate from the timed comparison.

## 1. CF-001: contract-backed working capital

**UI setup:** CF-001 → **Comprehensive**; keep all three sample triggers selected. Use **Case-linked materials** and **Follow-up questions**. The sample has a draft contract, an estimated property value and a working-capital request; these do not establish receipt/review status for the mapped rows.

**Task:** Ask about valuation contingency, evidence for the new work and the use/timing of funds. Identify what to seek first and what needs broker confirmation before arranging it.

**Observer key:**

- `valuation_sensitive` · Critical: lower valuation, extra equity or reduced borrowing. Mapping: **formal valuation; AVM; cash-to-complete; equity buffer**.
- `contract_growth` · Important: confirmed work and payment terms. Mapping: **signed contract; accepted quote; purchase order; payment terms**.
- `working_capital_vague` · Important: funds by category and timing. Mapping: **use-of-funds breakdown; supplier invoices; contract mobilisation budget; cash-flow forecast**.

Expected scope: **12 mapped names / 3 triggers / no conditional items**, not 12 mandatory documents. The existing contract rule allows a signed contract, accepted quote **or** purchase order; do not demand all three. Confirm the valuation route with the broker before ordering/paying. Cash-to-complete/equity buffer can be information to establish.

**Optional receipt check, on both versions after timing:** Say, “An accepted quote has arrived; nobody has reviewed it.” Tick **Record receipt only (not reviewed)** for **accepted quote**. Expected: it moves to **Received — awaiting document review**. Relevance, adequacy, alternatives and payment terms still need review; the Re-rate tracker, rating and confidence remain unchanged. No real file is supplied.

## 2. CF-002: a crowded Simple view

**UI setup:** CF-002 → **Simple**; retain all seven sample triggers. Do not switch tiers during timing. Its ATO screenshot and three months of statements do not establish whether the requested full records are available or reviewed.

**Task:** Pick three questions, explain their priority, identify this tier's omitted scope and distinguish applicant records from broker-coordinated work.

**Observer key, engine display order:** All six shown triggers are Critical. A different explained order is acceptable.

- `mca_present`: payout, retain or restructure the advance? **MCA agreement; current balance; payout letter; repayment history**.
- `dishonours_recent`: what happened and is conduct history available? **6–12 months business bank statements; explanation for each dishonour/excess**.
- `high_lvr_second_mortgage`: what indicative security, pricing and exit information exists? **private lender indicative terms; valuation/AVM; exit strategy; pricing estimate**. Ask the broker; do not assume any lender pathway exists.
- `ato_debt`: full account and plan records? **ATO integrated client account; ATO payment-plan letter; latest BAS lodgement status**.
- `missed_ato_plan`: amount, reason and catch-up timing? **payment-plan history; bank statement showing catch-up payment; accountant explanation**.
- `sg_unevidenced`: evidence of super payments? **SG clearing-house receipts; payroll reports; ATO/SBSCH or fund payment confirmation**.

Expected scope: **19 names / 6 Critical questions**. Simple exceeds its nominal five-question target to retain all selected Critical questions. It omits selected Important `working_capital_vague` and **use-of-funds breakdown; supplier invoices; contract mobilisation budget; cash-flow forecast**. The omission should be disclosed, not interpreted as resolution. No receipt controls, applicant draft or conditional expansion appear in Simple.

Do not equate the screenshot with a full ATO account, or three months with the rule's **6–12 months** request. Do not assume longer statements are absent either: ask whether already held. This period comes from the existing Oney rule, not a verified lender policy.

**Optional scope check:** After both timed runs, switch each version to Comprehensive: the working-capital question and four names appear, giving **7 questions / 23 names**. Record separately; do not score Simple against Comprehensive content.

## 3. CF-004: property evidence and applicability

**UI setup:** CF-004 → **Comprehensive**. Open **Related-party security or property-owning entity** in selected triggers and uncheck it. Keep the other three triggers and all intake unchanged, identically in A and B. This deliberately creates an unconfirmed suggestion; it does not establish absence of a related-party issue. The edited scenario may be labelled custom.

**Task:** Ask about tenant departure, property due diligence and valuation contingency. Explain whether to request related-party/SMSF material immediately and who should clarify applicability.

**Observer key:**

- `short_wale` · Critical: servicing if the tenant leaves? **full lease; tenant payment history; servicing sensitivity; vacancy/re-leasing plan**.
- `zoning_environmental_missing` · Critical: confirmed use and known environmental issues? **zoning certificate; permitted-use confirmation; environmental risk check; contract due-diligence clauses**.
- `valuation_sensitive` · Critical: lower valuation and equity/borrowing contingency? **formal valuation; AVM; cash-to-complete; equity buffer**.
- `related_party_security` · Important, **suggested but unselected**: clarify ownership, borrower and any related-party lease, guarantee or SMSF involvement. Conditional mapping: **title/ownership details; entity structure; related-party lease; guarantee/security consent; SMSF confirmation**.

Expected scope: **12 selected names + 5 conditional names**. Conditional rows have no receipt checkbox. They are not a blanket applicant request; “SMSF confirmation” does not establish that an SMSF exists. Servicing sensitivity is broker-coordinated work. The rent-roll summary does not establish review of a full lease/payment history. Confirm report scope/provider with the broker; invent no report standard or lender policy.

**Optional applicability check:** Select the related-party trigger in both versions after timing. Its five names move from Conditional to Needed; total stays **17**. The selected trigger set changed, not a verified lender requirement or underwriting verdict. Input changes clear any receipt notes; do not carry them into the changed case.

## Shared observation sheet and rubric

Record A and B separately; use “not observed” where appropriate:

- Next three questions and mapped evidence/reason: 1. ___ 2. ___ 3. ___
- Overlooked or falsely settled evidence, with exact rule/item: ___
- Unnecessary/premature requests: ___; cause: alternative / conditional / wrong provider / unsupported policy claim
- Requirement source understood: Yes / Partly / No; tester's explanation: ___
- Possible provider and remaining uncertainty understood: Yes / Partly / No; example: ___
- Clarity: __/5. Elapsed: __ seconds. Hints: __. Hesitation/quote: ___
- Optional check result or unexpected behaviour: ___

Score each proposed question **0** (unmapped/irrelevant), **1** (relevant but evidence/reason unclear), or **2** (selected-trigger question with appropriate mapped evidence/reason), maximum 6. Accept paraphrases and explained ordering. Questions outside the chosen three are not automatically omissions. Log an omission when a tester dismisses a relevant mapped issue or cannot find/interpret it when probed. Separate participant omissions, screen omissions and deliberate tier scope. Do not penalise a broker-confirmed alternative because every label was not requested.

The new guidance should make these distinctions understandable: **general preparation, no lender-confirmed source supplied; possible provider to confirm; unrecorded receipt means availability unknown; received means review still needed; conditional means applicability unconfirmed**. Neither receipt nor item count establishes a sufficient pack or lending outcome.

Report raw paired observations and practice/order effects, including regressions. Do not turn a small internal test into claims of completeness, lender acceptance, time savings or readiness for release.
