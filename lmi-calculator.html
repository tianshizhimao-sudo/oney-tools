Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI)?

LMI is a one-off insurance premium that protects the lender (not you) if you default on your home loan. It's typically required when your deposit is less than 20% of the property value. The cost is usually added to your loan balance.

How much does LMI cost in Australia?

LMI costs vary based on your loan-to-value ratio (LVR) and loan amount. For example, on a $600,000 property with a 10% deposit, LMI could range from $8,000 to $15,000 depending on the lender. The higher your LVR, the more you'll pay.

Can I avoid paying LMI?

Yes. The most common way is saving a 20% deposit. You can also use a family guarantee, access the First Home Guarantee scheme (which allows 5% deposit with no LMI), or choose a lender that offers LMI waivers for certain professionals like doctors and lawyers.

Is LMI refundable if I sell or refinance?

In some cases, yes. If you sell or refinance within the first few years, you may be eligible for a partial LMI refund. Policies vary by insurer — check with your lender or broker for details.
