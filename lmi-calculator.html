What is LMI? Lenders Mortgage Insurance protects the lender (not you) when your deposit is less than 20%. It's a one-off premium added to your loan or paid upfront. This tool provides estimates only — actual premiums vary by lender.

💡 Ways to Reduce or Avoid LMI

  • Save a 20% deposit to avoid LMI entirely
  • Use a guarantor (family pledge) to boost your deposit
  • Check if you qualify for First Home Guarantee (no LMI for eligible buyers)
  • Some professions (doctors, lawyers, accountants) may get LMI waivers
  • Compare lenders — LMI premiums vary significantly
